# Strid

Strid is an iOS app that identifies French orthoptera — crickets, grasshoppers,
bush-crickets — by their song.

## Philosophy

**Everything stays on your device.** Recognition runs on an embedded acoustic
model, the species catalogue ships inside the app, and your recordings and
observations are stored locally. Strid works entirely offline: no account, no
analytics, no ads, no network requests.

**Privacy is the default, not a setting.** The microphone is only active while
you record. Location is optional and off by default; when enabled, an
approximate position is attached at the moment of capture and never leaves the
device. Deleting your data — one observation or all of it — is a single tap.

**Built on open data.** The species catalogue derives from TAXREF (PatriNat /
MNHN), observation statistics from GBIF, and photographs from iNaturalist
contributors under CC0 / CC BY licenses, with per-photo credit shown in the
app. The training data behind the acoustic model keeps per-recording
provenance and license.
